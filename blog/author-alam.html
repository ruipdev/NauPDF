Alam

Creator and maintainer of pdffdp

I’m Alam, and I build pdffdp, a free, open-source PDF toolkit with 100+ tools that run entirely in your browser.

There’s no uploading your files to a server, no account to create, and no tracking. Your documents stay on your device. The source code is public, so you can see how it works yourself. pdffdp is AGPL-licensed and self-hostable.

I write every post on this blog myself. I’ll write about PDFs, how they work, things I learn while building pdffdp, and occasionally things that go wrong.

When I compare pdffdp with another PDF tool, I’ll link to its documentation so you can check what I’m saying. If there’s something pdffdp doesn’t do well, I’ll say that too.

And if I get something wrong, please open an issue. I’ll fix it and add a note to the post.

You can find me on GitHub, LinkedIn and X.
